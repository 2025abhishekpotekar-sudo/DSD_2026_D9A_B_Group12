## 2. Individual performance
Assess each student separately for:
- Understanding of the complete architecture and design decisions.
- Ability to explain FSM/Logisim work, where applicable.
- Understanding of Verilog RTL and testbench.
- Ability to interpret simulation and hardware results.
- Actual contribution, collaboration and professional conduct.

Use `Peer_Review_Template.md` as supporting evidence. Peer review informs, but does not automatically determine, faculty marks.

## 5. Evaluation notes
- Apply the same rubric consistently to all groups.
- Distinguish group-level quality from individual understanding.
- Ask questions of different members, not only the group leader.
- Verify claims against source files, simulation evidence and hardware demonstration.
- Record incomplete work and limitations fairly.
